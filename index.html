Why IRON exists

IRON exists to help young people draw closer to God, grow in their faith, and feel encouraged to share the Good News. We want to be a community that supports, prays for, and encourages one another while living according to God’s will.

Our purpose follows the call Jesus gave His followers: go, make disciples, and stay close to Him as we do.

The Great Commission

“Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.”

Matthew 28:19–20 (NIV)

About IRON

IRON is a youth fellowship built on a simple idea: we grow stronger in faith when we grow together.

Our name comes from Proverbs 27:17. Iron is sharpened by iron, and in the same way we help each other become more faithful, more caring, and more ready to serve. Whether you’ve walked with God for years or are just starting to ask questions, there’s a place for you here.

We meet to pray, learn from Scripture, encourage one another, and live out our faith.

What we value

Faith

Trusting God and building our lives on His Word.

Fellowship

Praying for, listening to, and supporting each other.

Growth

Becoming more like Christ, one step at a time.

Service

Using our time and gifts to help and bless others.

Discipleship

Following Jesus and helping others follow Him too.
